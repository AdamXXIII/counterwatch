- The 23 Omzhh cases (challenge_rule: true) are his own calls under a self-imposed rule (counter-swap every enemy tank). They show which counter he picks, not whether a swap was wise. Do not score 'should I swap' against them.
- Spilo's two swap-guide cases are worked examples from a 2023 guide, not live moments.
